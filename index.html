arenal semi-modular pattern synth

Patching

Drag from any jack to another to patch. Outputs sit on the dark plates. A cable into an input replaces the route printed under it (normalled routes, so the synth plays with no cables at all).

Drag a cable's input end away to re-patch it, drop it on empty panel to unplug it, or double-click the cable. Feedback loops are allowed.

Pattern recorder

Hold REC and the tracked note of OSC A is written on every falling edge of the clock. On release the length snaps to 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 48 or 64 steps (3, 12 and 24 are kept). Modulate OSC A while recording to capture melodies from LFOs, STRATA or feedback.

A / B start, switch and stop the two patterns. QUANT pulls OSC A onto the nearest note of the selected scale. TUNE transposes playback.

Keyboard

A–; play OSC A (white keys), W E T Y U O P sharps, Z/X octave. Hold Space to record, 1/2 for pattern A/B, Q toggles QUANT.

Patches store every knob, switch, cable and both patterns. Export writes them to a file you can import later or share.

Record on the OUTPUT module captures the main output as a 24-bit WAV at the audio device's sample rate.

Knobs: drag up or down (hold Shift for fine), scroll, arrow keys, double-click to reset.
